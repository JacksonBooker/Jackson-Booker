---
title: Solving Inequalities
---

You solve inequalities as you would like any other equation. Here is an example:

```math
\begin{align*}
2x + 3 &> 7 \\
2x &> 4 \\
x &> 2
\end{align*}
```

As you can see we did this just like a normal equation. 

## Flipping Sign

You now might see something like this, where each side is being dived by a negative number. Anytime there is dividing or multiplying by a negative number you switch the inequality to the opposite direction. See example:

```math
\[ \frac{-3x}{5} \geq -4 \]
\[ -3x \geq -20 \]
\[ x \leq \frac{20}{3} \]
```
