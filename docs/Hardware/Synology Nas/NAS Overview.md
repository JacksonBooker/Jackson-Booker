This is my current NAS or Network Attached Storage. This product is a large data storage bin that can be accessed through local network. I bought this NAS for around *$600* and bought two HDD hard drives for *$110* each. The hard drives were Seagate Wolf 4TB each. I am using this as my homelab server, nothing fancy but reliable. The NAS works especially great when ethernet is attached and to the client it is connecting to.

### Hard Drives
This NAS allows 4 HDD drives and 2 NVMe SSD caching drives on the bottom of the device to make it faster. I have not personally added them yet but that would be my next venture. 

### Remote Access
One of the biggest challenges people will face with a NAS is remote access. A solution to this is an application called [[tailscale]]. Tailscale is free for home users and there goal is for you to eventually proceed to buy the paid plan at your work or office.
